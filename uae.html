Immigration Pathways

Long-term residency pathways for the UAE

The UAE doesn't offer citizenship-by-immigration, but it has strong long-term residency routes.

UAE Golden Visa

10-year renewable residency for investors, skilled professionals, outstanding students and specialized talents.

Green Visa

5-year self-sponsored residency for freelancers, skilled employees and business owners, without an employer sponsor.

Property Investor Visa

Long-term residency route tied to qualifying real estate investment in the UAE.

Retirement Visa

5-year renewable residency for retirees meeting the UAE's savings, income or property criteria.

Program names and eligibility criteria are set by UAE federal and emirate-level authorities and change periodically — we'll confirm current requirements during your consultation.
