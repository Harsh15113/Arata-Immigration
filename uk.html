Immigration Pathways

Settlement & PR pathway programs for the UK

Most UK work and family visas lead toward Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) — permanent settlement in the UK.

Skilled Worker Visa → ILR

The most common employer-sponsored route, typically leading to settlement after 5 continuous years.

UK Global Talent Visa

Fast-track route for leaders and emerging talent in academia, arts, digital technology and research.

Innovator Founder Visa

For experienced entrepreneurs setting up an innovative, scalable UK business.

UK Ancestry Visa

For Commonwealth citizens with a UK-born grandparent, offering a direct route to work and later settle.

Family / Spouse Visa → ILR

Partner and family sponsorship routes that lead to settlement after the qualifying period.

UK Investor-Linked Routes

Guidance on current business and investment-linked visa options as UK immigration rules evolve.

Program names and eligibility criteria are set by UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) and change periodically — we'll confirm current requirements during your consultation.
