Immigration Pathways

Green Card & PR pathway programs for the USA

The US offers employment, investment and family-based routes to permanent residency. We help you find the right one.

Employment-Based Green Card (EB-1/EB-2/EB-3)

Routes for priority workers, advanced-degree professionals and skilled workers sponsored by a US employer.

EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program

Permanent residency through a qualifying investment in a US commercial enterprise that creates jobs.

Family-Based Green Card

Sponsorship routes for immediate relatives and other family-preference categories.

Diversity Visa (DV) Lottery

Annual lottery-based Green Card program for applicants from countries with historically low US immigration rates.

Adjustment of Status

Support transitioning eligible non-immigrant visa holders already in the US to permanent resident status.

Program names and eligibility criteria are set by USCIS and the US Department of State and change periodically — we'll confirm current requirements during your consultation.
